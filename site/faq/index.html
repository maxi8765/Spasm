Frequently asked questions

Is SPASM free?

Yes. Everything in the app is free. If your name is missing, you can pay for David to write a personal write-up for it.

Do I need to download anything?

No. SPASM runs in your phone's browser at https://app.spasm.pro. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like any other app. See how to install.

How many names are in SPASM?

5,319: 3,013 female and 2,306 male.

Who writes the name descriptions?

David Hocking, author of Why Shouldn't I Call My Son Clint? Every write-up is his.

My name is missing. Can I add it?

Yes. Search for it in the app and SPASM offers to add it.

Is it accurate?

It is meant to be fun. Read your own write-up and decide for yourself.

What languages does it support?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese and Hindi.

Does SPASM collect my data?

SPASM keeps your saved name and favourites on your own phone. See the privacy policy.

Try it on your own name

SPASM is free and works on any phone. No sign-up, no app store needed.
