The nine personality types

Every name in SPASM belongs to one of nine personality types, worked out from its Hocko score. Pick a type to see who belongs to it and who they get on with.

Type 1: The Reformer

Principled, purposeful and self-controlled, with a strong sense of right and wrong.

726 names

Type 2: The Helper

Warm, generous and people-pleasing, happiest when needed.

579 names

Type 3: The Achiever

Driven, adaptable and image-conscious, built for success.

Sensitive, expressive and self-aware, sometimes moody.

Type 5: The Investigator

Curious, private and inventive, more at home with ideas than small talk.

Committed, responsible and security-minded, quick to spot trouble.

Easygoing, accepting and reassuring, avoids conflict at all costs.
