How SPASM works

Your name is the first label anyone gives you, and you hear it every day for the rest of your life. David Hocking's idea is that the way people react to a name slowly rubs off on the person carrying it. SPASM turns that idea into numbers.

Ten traits

David scored every name in SPASM on ten traits:

  • Charisma
  • Spontaneity
  • Emotional savvy
  • Sexual adventurousness
  • Motivation
  • Creativity
  • Humour
  • Spirituality
  • Leadership
  • Generosity

Charisma is scored out of 10. The other nine traits are scored out of 5, because in David's view charisma counts about twice as much as everything else.

The Hocko score

Add the ten scores together and you get the Hocko score. Across all 5,319 names it runs from 6 to 51. The Hocko score decides which of nine personality types a name belongs to.

Nine personality types

The nine types follow the Enneagram, a personality model that has been around since the 1960s and 70s. Each type has a best and worst partner type, which drives SPASM's compatibility and kiss, marry or avoid results. See all nine types.

Putting it together

Two names give two personality types and two adventurer types. From those SPASM reads off a couple score from a 9 by 9 table, a bedroom score from a 5 by 5 table, the best personality type for your children, and the people you should kiss, marry or avoid. David wrote 405 couple write-ups and 150 bedroom write-ups to go with the scores.

The full method is in David's paper, written in the style of a scientific journal and best read with a straight face.
