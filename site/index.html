What does your name say about you?

SPASM reads your first name and tells you who you are. It also tells you who you should be with, what to call your kids, and who to kiss, marry or avoid.

5,319 names, every one written by a human

Every write-up in SPASM was written by David Hocking, author of Why Shouldn't I Call My Son Clint? That's 3,013 female names and 2,306 male names, from Aaron to Zoe, plus nicknames and a good number of names you have probably never met. No write-up was generated by a computer.

Is this science?

Sort of. People really do match faces to names better than chance, and teachers really do expect more from children with names they like. SPASM takes that idea a long way further than any scientist would. Read how it works or David's tongue-in-cheek scientific paper, then decide for yourself.

Works in five languages

SPASM runs in English, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese and Hindi, and picks your phone's language automatically.
