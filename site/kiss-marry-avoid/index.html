Kiss, marry or avoid

Give SPASM your name and it gives you seven names to kiss, seven to marry and seven to steer well clear of.

Marry

Names to marry come from your personality type's best partner type, and only make the list if your bedroom score together is above 61%.

Avoid

Names to avoid come from your worst partner type, and only if your bedroom score is 60% or lower. SPASM won't tell you to avoid someone you'd at least have fun with.

Kiss

Names to kiss share your adventurer type, whatever their personality. That's how kissing generally works.

Try it on your own name
