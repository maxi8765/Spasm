Name compatibility

Put two first names into SPASM and you get two scores: how well you work as a couple, and how well you work in the bedroom. Each comes with a write-up by David Hocking.

The couple score

The couple score comes from the two names' personality types. There are 81 possible pairings, scoring from 10% to 100%. Eleven pairings score a perfect 100% and nine score 10%. Each pairing has five write-ups, and SPASM picks one at random, so you can check again and get a different take.

The bedroom score

The bedroom score comes from the two names' adventurer types. Matching types score 100%, and the score drops 20% for every step apart. A Homebody and an Explorer score 20% together, which seems about right.
