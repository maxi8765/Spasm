What your name says about you

Most name sites tell you where a name came from. SPASM tells you what it's like to be one. Here are a few of the 5,319 write-ups in the app.

Ian (male)

The sound of a teaspoon being dropped onto a tiled floor. The ungainly twang is noisy without being painful and more often than not it leaves a mess, but did you ever stop and study the shape of the pattern of the mess? Check it out next time. You might be surprised to discover the artist that lies within. Suffice to say if Ian is ever undergoing some kind of existential crisis you’ll never know about it unless he tells you.

Nicola (female)

Intense and solemn like an encyclopaedia in a library, Nicola means well but she has twisted ideas about men and about the world. With lots of unconditional love, however, she’ll be fine.

David (male)

David is more serious than he appears because the heavy D’s at both ends weigh his name down like the legs of a North Sea oil platform, and to this end he may be too deep and sensitive for his own good. However, the ‘V’ in the middle provides a powerful launch pad for virtually anything. We wish him well.

Dennis (male)

Not exactly a rogue elephant, Dennis is more like a rogue daffodil, with the occasional exception of Dennis the Alcoholic.

Charlotte (female)

Blessed with incredible luck, both perfect and farcical, Charlotte is a gifted lover, sister and partier, who enjoys nothing more than sitting around at an outdoor restaurant with her multitude of friends, while they feast on good food and wine in the summer sun. Outstanding performance, Miss Charlotte.

Jack (male)

As one of life’s great experiences, Jack is the name given to the phenomenon of a parcel arriving unexpectedly in the mail from a good friend. Outstanding. (Note: Jack is another Child Nightmare.)

Olivia (female)

Should we describe Olivia as a drama queen? Indeed, is it fair to describe anyone as drama queen? We think it is. Why? Because if no one cried out and carried on, needlessly garnering attention and diverting the spotlight, how boring would life be? Imagine living in a world where no one complained, where no one shouted their euphoria from the top of a mountain at the top of their lungs? It’d be like everyone living in Switzerland where nothing outlandish happens, where all trains run on time and where no one is late for work. Life, remember, is a collected blend of light and shade, of noise and heat, of humour and tragedy, and thank Christ people like Olivia here exist, is all we can say. So, does Olivia carry on and sulk for no reason sometimes? …Sure. Does she get excited over the littlest things? …Yep. Would life be dull without her? …I bloody-well guarantee it. Amen.

Oliver (male)

Once we described Oliver is serious and sensitive, and someone who was in his element when it rained for days on end because it meant he didn’t have to leave the house. A little confession from the boys in the commentary box….we were just trying to get a rise out of him. Oliver is sensitive, that part is true enough, and sure, he’s serious when he needs to be, just, but Oliver is into life and he has a fun streak which runs through the middle of his entire being. You may not necessarily see it (although in the case of Oliver being the loudest and most extravagant personality in the room, you can’t miss it) but be rest assured that somewhere, somehow, perhaps in a social setting which you aren’t privy to yet, Oliver is the life of the gathering and people are disappointed if he doesn’t show up. Too right.

Sarah (female)

A prototype model of perfection. She tries not to offend people but sometimes it can’t be helped. Thoughtful, philosophical, generous and very sexy on her day, Sarah is generally an impatient cook.

Michael (male)

Catholic school boy. Political advisor/fundraiser. Head engineer. Chief Justice. Airline pilot.....he’s in there somewhere*. *(ie- he’s organised, he’s smart and he’s reasonably conservative.)

Emma (female)

Like the surface of a swollen river after the monsoon, she’s smooth and bubbling on the surface, but all those m’s are almost too perfect to be true because beneath them is a chaotic riverbed of drama and complexity. Befriend one and you must ask yourself the question: Can I swim?

James (male)

A master of disguise and one of the Enigmas. Fiendishly clever, the only thing he won’t do is dig holes, although he does loathe all forms of menial labour. Occasionally very serious, rarely flippant and often the funniest person in a room, he always keeps a little in reserve. This is the James’ way.
