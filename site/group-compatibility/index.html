Group compatibility

Add the names of your friends, family, housemates or the people in your next meeting, and SPASM tells you how well the group gets on.

Friendship, not romance

Group scores are about friendship. Each pair's score blends their personality-type compatibility with how well their traits fit together, weighted 60% type and 40% traits.

The grid

Every pair in the group gets a square on the grid, coloured green for great, yellow for good, orange for patchy and red for trouble. The group gets a final score and a verdict, so you know whether to book the holiday house.

Try it on your own name

SPASM is free and works on any phone. No sign-up, no app store needed.
