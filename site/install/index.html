Install SPASM on your phone

SPASM is a web app. It installs straight from the browser and opens like any other app.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open https://spasm.pages.dev in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to home screen, then Install.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open https://spasm.pages.dev in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Try it on your own name

SPASM is free and works on any phone. No sign-up, no app store needed.
