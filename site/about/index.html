About SPASM

SPASM is the app version of David Hocking's book Why Shouldn't I Call My Son Clint?

The book's premise is that every name gets a small, half-conscious reaction from the people we meet, and over a lifetime those reactions shape who we become.

SPASM takes the book's write-ups and adds the scoring, the compatibility tables, the baby names and the games. Every write-up is still David's own.
