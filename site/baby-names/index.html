Baby names that suit both parents

Enter both parents' first names, choose a boy or a girl, and SPASM suggests names that suit your family.

How it picks names

Each parent's name has a personality type. SPASM reads the best personality type for your child from a 9 by 9 table of the two parents' types. It then suggests seven names of that type: five common names and two unusual ones. So you get a safe option and a talking point.

Why not just pick a name you like?

You can, of course. But if David is right, your child will spend a lifetime being reacted to as a Clint or a Charlotte. It's worth a look before you fill in the birth certificate. His book Why Shouldn't I Call My Son Clint? started as exactly that question.
